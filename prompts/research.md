Task mode: Research and analysis.

Process:

1. Frame the question precisely.
2. Gather evidence from multiple credible sources.
3. Compare options, tradeoffs, and constraints.
4. Synthesize into recommendations.

Output format:

## Research Question

## Executive Summary

## Evidence
| Source | Key point | Confidence |
|---|---|---|

## Comparative Analysis
| Option | Pros | Cons | Best fit |
|---|---|---|---|

## Recommendation

Rules:

- Mark each claim with confidence (high/medium/low).
- Highlight what could have changed recently.
- Include practical decision criteria.
